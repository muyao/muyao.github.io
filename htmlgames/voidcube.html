INSTRUCTIONS

General Controls

  • Movement: WASD or ↑←↓→
  • Reset: R
  • Back to menu: Green Flag

Types of jumps:

  • High: Press and hold W
  • Light: Tap W
  • High Hop: Press and hold W while pressing S
  • Light Hop: Tap W while pressing S
  • You move the fastest while hopping, which is useful
  • for speedrunning...

Editor Controls

  • Activate Flight: E
  • Deactivate Flight: T
  • Undo: U
  • Cancel Platform: O
  • To add a platform, drag your mouse from one point to another, then press Enter.
  • Switch between different platform types using 1 - 5:
    1. Basic
    2. Jump-pad
    3. Goal
    4. Anti-jump
    5. Snail
  • Press and hold S + Enter to save your level. MAKE SURE YOU COPY THE WHOLE SAVE CODE!
  • Press and hold L + Enter to load a level to edit. Leave it blank to cancel.
  • This is different to the "Load Level" button, which is not in editor mode.

Platform Types

  • Basic: Just the basic blue platform
  • Jump-pad: Launches the player up. Height increases by 10% every bounce.
  • Anti-jump: Prevents the player from jumping
  • Snail: Slows the player down
